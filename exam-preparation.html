Exam Preparation

Preparation habits that work for any exam

Good exam preparation isn't about cramming — it's about a few simple habits, used consistently. Here's what we recommend, whatever exam is coming up.

Revision techniques

Four habits that make revision stick

01

Little and often

Short, regular sessions beat long, rare ones. Spacing practice out over weeks helps it stay in memory.

02

Practice recalling, not just reading

Testing yourself — even with simple questions — builds stronger recall than re-reading notes.

03

Practice under real conditions

Timed practice papers build comfort with pacing and pressure, so exam day feels familiar.

04

Rest is part of the plan

Sleep and breaks aren't a break from revising — they're what makes revising actually work.

Exam-day confidence

Feeling ready when the day arrives

A calm exam day starts well before the exam itself. A few simple habits go a long way.

The week before

Review key areas lightly rather than starting new topics. Keep sleep and routine as normal as possible.

The night before

Lay out what's needed, do something calming, and get an early night. Cramming late rarely helps.

On the day

Eat a proper breakfast, arrive with time to spare, and read every question carefully before answering.
