School Support

Steady support, whatever year they're in

Not every family is thinking about the 11+ — some just want steady, general support for school. This page covers the broad picture; more detailed subject and year-group pages are on the way.

By year group

Support that fits where they are now

Year 2–4

Building solid foundations in reading, number sense and confidence — the groundwork everything later builds on.

Year 5–6

Strengthening core skills and study habits, including for families with the 11+ or secondary transfer in mind.

Secondary school

Support as new subjects, harder material and more independent study arrive all at once.

Looking for a specific subject or year group in detail? That's exactly the kind of page we're building next — get in touch and let us know what would help most.

Areas of support

The four things we focus on

Reading & comprehension

Understanding what's read, not just decoding it — the skill behind almost every other subject.

Maths & reasoning

Building number confidence and the kind of logical thinking used in verbal and non-verbal reasoning.

Confidence & study habits

Helping children believe in their own ability, and building routines that make studying feel manageable.

Exam readiness

General preparation and technique that applies well beyond any single exam. See our dedicated page for more.
