    Q1

    Who we are

    We started MyExamHub because getting ready for the 11+ shouldn't feel like guesswork, for parents or for children. We're building clear, calm resources that explain what to expect and when, without the jargon or the overwhelm exam season usually brings.

    Q2

    What we do

    Three things, done properly rather than ten things done thinly:

    • a)

      Exam preparation — revision techniques and habits that build real understanding, not last-minute cramming.

    • b)

      School support — everyday academic support across year groups, not just the exam itself.

    • c)

      11+ resources — mock exams, practice papers and courses that mirror the real thing.

    Q3

    What these resources help with

    Every resource is built around one outcome for your family: the real exam feels like practice, not a surprise.

    Familiarity

    Children get comfortable with the exam format and pacing well before the day itself.

    Clarity

    Plain-English guidance for parents on what to expect and when, no expertise required.

    Pace

    Preparation broken into manageable steps, so progress feels achievable, not rushed.
