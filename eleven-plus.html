Tips for success

What actually helps

01

Start early, go steady

Consistent, unhurried practice over months beats a rushed sprint in the final weeks.

02

Practise the format

Familiarity with question types and timing matters as much as raw ability.

03

Review mistakes, not just scores

Understanding why an answer was wrong builds more progress than the mark alone.

04

Protect their confidence

A calm, encouraged child performs better than an anxious, over-drilled one.

Frequently asked questions

Common 11+ questions

What is the 11+ exam?

The 11+ is an entrance exam some children take around age 11 to gain a place at certain secondary schools. It typically covers areas such as verbal reasoning, non-verbal reasoning, maths and English.

When should my child start preparing?

There's no single right answer — it depends on your child and your target schools. Many families begin gentle preparation in Year 4 or 5, building up steadily rather than starting intensively all at once.

What do the mock exams involve?

Mock exams are designed to feel like the real thing: timed conditions, exam-style questions, and a debrief afterwards so your child (and you) know exactly where to focus next.

Can I book a place online yet?

Online booking is coming soon. For now, get in touch and we'll help you find the right mock exam or course.

What do the practice papers cover?

Verbal reasoning, non-verbal reasoning, maths and English comprehension — the core areas most 11+ exams draw from.
